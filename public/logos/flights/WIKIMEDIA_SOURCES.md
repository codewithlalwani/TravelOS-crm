# Wikimedia Commons regional airline logos

Source collection: [Logos of airlines](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Logos_of_airlines), including its country and airline-name subcategories.

Downloaded on 2026-08-13 and grouped for application use:

- `gulf/`: Air Arabia, flyadeal, flynas, Gulf Air, Jazeera Airways, Oman Air
- `india/`: Air India Express, Akasa Air, Fly91, SpiceJet, Star Air
- `canada/`: Air Canada, Air Inuit, Air Transat, Flair Airlines, Porter Airlines, WestJet
- `usa/`: Alaska Airlines, Frontier Airlines
- `australia/`: Air Australia, Jetstar

The existing top-level flight library already includes major carriers such as Air India, IndiGo, Emirates, Etihad, Qatar Airways, American, Delta, Southwest, United, and Qantas, so those were not duplicated here.

Each downloaded file's Commons description page can be found by searching its airline name in the source collection. Wikimedia files have individual licensing terms; logos may also be protected trademarks. Confirm the description page and brand guidelines before production use.
